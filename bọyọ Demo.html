Interactive demo

This runs the whole platform flow inside your browser: an organizer uploads a flyer, the event page takes its colours, people register and get a QR ticket, the gate checks them in (even offline), and the organizer watches turnout. Nothing leaves this page. The real app does this on a server with phone cameras at the gate.

Set up the event

Upload a flyer and the colours are picked from it. Tech Ignite '26 is loaded as an example. Change anything and open the Event page tab to see it.

Pick the date from your Google Calendar

Front desk logins

Give each person on your information desk their own login. They can look people up, check them in and scan, but cannot change the event, export data or manage logins. Removing a login signs that person out at once.

Expected guests

Upload a CSV of the people you are expecting. Each one shows as registered or checked in once they sign up with the same email, so you can see who is missing and keep the names for your programme.

Headings such as Name, Email, Organization, Role. Only a name is required. From Excel: Save as, then CSV.
